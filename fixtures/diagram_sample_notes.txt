Procurement intake process notes, workshop 3 June.

Requester: Submit a purchase request with the item details and cost estimate.
Requester: Attach three vendor quotes when the value is above 5000 dollars.
Procurement analyst: Review the purchase request for completeness in Coupa.
Procurement analyst: Return incomplete requests to the requester with comments.
Finance controller: Check the budget availability for the cost center in SAP.
Finance controller: Approve or reject the request based on the budget check.
Procurement analyst: Create the purchase order in SAP and send it to the vendor.
Vendor: Confirm the purchase order and communicate the delivery date.
Warehouse team: Receive the goods and post the goods receipt in SAP.
Accounts payable: Match the invoice against the purchase order and goods receipt.
Accounts payable: Release the payment according to the agreed payment terms.
